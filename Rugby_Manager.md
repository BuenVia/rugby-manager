### Rugby Manager

## Concept

A rugby management simulator in the style of the Football Manager games.

User signs as a Head Coach of a team and manages their training, tactics and in-game tactics. 

This will handle international rugby initially, starting with the 6 Nations, later adding in Rugby Champions, Rugby Europe Championship, Pacific Nations Cup, World Cup and Rugby Champions Cup competitions.

---

## Tech Stack

Java 21
Spring Boot 4
React
